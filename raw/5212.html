Hearing Details Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government

Search the Legislature

Event Details

Status:
Completed
Event Date:
Tuesday, June 10, 2025
Start Time:
1:00 PM
Location:
B-1 and Virtual
Event Description
Animals, Governance, Enforcement, and Personnel, Administration and Finance, Home Rules

The Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government will hold a hybrid public hearing to accept written and oral testimony on the below-listed legislation. This hearing will be chaired by Senate Chair Becca Rausch. Please be advised that the docket is subject to change by agreement of the Chairs. 

 

Persons seeking to provide oral testimony, whether in person or remotely, are asked to pre-register by 10:00 a.m. on Monday, June 9, 2025, using this online form: https://forms.office.com/g/XzKWtJXPMY. Persons seeking to testify remotely must pre-register by the stated deadline; further instructions on how to participate will be provided after registration. Oral testimony is limited to 3 minutes per person. Oral testimony from panels comprised of four (4) or more individuals is limited to 10 minutes total, to be distributed at the discretion of the panelists. Individuals and panels may testify only once, and testimony may cover multiple bills; all testimony must be completed within the stated time limits. 

 

Written testimony does not require pre-registration and may be submitted via email to: jointcommittee.municipalities&regionalgovernment@malegislature.gov. Written testimony should be submitted not later than two (2) weeks following the hearing date. 

 

All above-listed matters filed in the House are required to be reported on by August 9, 2025, subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27. 

 

The public is invited to participate in this hybrid hearing, which will be livestreamed on the General Court website: https://malegislature.gov/. Hearings will be recorded and posted publicly on the Committee page: https://malegislature.gov/Committees/Detail/J10/Hearings. 

 

At the discretion of the Chairs and per committee rules, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. The committee may limit availability or redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information, information about minors, or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness, or safety of the testifier or others.

 

The Committee strongly prefers that written testimony be submitted by email, as specified above. If email submission is not possible, written testimony may be submitted in hard copy to: 

 

Senator Becca Rausch, Chair 

Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government 

State House Room 215 

Boston, MA 02133 

 

Questions regarding the hearing, legislation, or reasonable accommodations to ensure people with disabilities can participate fully in the committee process may be directed to Madelein McCormick with Chair Rausch at Madelein.McCormick@masenate.gov or 617-722-1555, or Geoff Richelew with Chair Lewis at Geoffrey.Richelew@mahouse.gov. 

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
