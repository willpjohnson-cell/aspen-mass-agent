10:00 AM
Location:
A-1 and Virtual
Event Description
Wage Theft, Prevailing Wage, Collective Bargaining

Wage Theft/Prevailing Wage/Collective Bargaining 

The public is invited to participate in The Joint Committee on Labor and Workforce Development hearing on Tuesday, October 28th at 10:00 a.m. in Hearing Room A-1.  This hearing will be chaired by Representative Paul McMurtry.

 

Registration: 

To register to testify in-person or virtually, please provide contact information via this FORM. The deadline to register is Monday, October 27th, at 1:00 p.m.  If you plan to testify as a panel, please note the name(s) of your co-panelist(s) on the form when registering. The livestream of this hearing will be available on the Legislature's website at https://malegislature.gov/. The Chairs will limit testimony to 3 minutes per speaker. Please be advised that the schedule and agenda are subject to change at the agreement of the Chairs.  

 

Hearings will be recorded and posted publicly on the Joint Committee on Labor and Workforce Development page Joint Committee on Labor and Workforce Development.  

 

Written Testimony: 

Written or pre-recorded testimony may be submitted until Tuesday, November 4th at 5:00 PM via email to labor.workforce.development@gmail.com.  

 
Written testimony can be physically mailed to the House Chair: 
Chair Paul McMurtry  
Joint Committee on Labor and Workforce Development 
ATTN: Garrett Burns Esq. 
24 BEACON ST. RM. 472  
BOSTON MA  02133 
   

All written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. At the discretion of the Chairs and per committee rules, limitations and redactions shall exist for sharing testimony that includes sensitive personal information or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness or safety of the testifier or others. Any person submitting written testimony may request such testimony remain private and said testimony will not be made publicly available.   

 

*All House matters above are required to be reported on by December 3, 2025, subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27.   

 

Contact: 

Please contact the Committee staff in advance if you are requesting any ADA accommodations or if you have any questions: 

Garrett.Burns@mahouse.gov 

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
