Hearing Details Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government

Search the Legislature

Event Details

Status:
Completed
Event Date:
Tuesday, June 24, 2025
Start Time:
1:00 PM
Location:
B-1 and Virtual
Event Description
Home rule petitions, local governance, municipal finance

The public is invited to participate in this hybrid hearing, which will be live streamed on the General Court website, https://malegislature.gov. Hearings will be recorded and posted publicly on the Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government page which is accessible at [https://malegislature.gov/Committees/Detail/J10].

Those wishing to testify remotely must pre-register by clicking on this link. All those wishing to testify in person may sign up on the day of the hearing but are encouraged to sign up ahead of time using the link above. Once registered, those planning to testify remotely will receive an email with a Microsoft Teams link and instructions prior to the start of the hearing.

Written testimony may be submitted via email to jointcommittee.municipalities&regionalgovernment@malegislature.gov or physically mailed to the committee:

Rep. Jack Patrick Lewis, House Chair

Joint Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government 

Massachusetts State House

Attn: Geoff Richelew

24 Beacon Street, Room 450

Boston, MA 02133

The committee respectfully asks that individuals use only one method to submit testimony. Please address your comments to both House Chair Lewis and Senate Chair Rebecca Rausch.

At the discretion of the chairs and per committee rules, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available by request. The committee may limit availability or redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information, information about minors, or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness, or safety of the testifier or others.

Please note that all matters on the agenda that were filed in the House will be subject to the reporting procedures in House Rule 27.

This hearing will be chaired by House Chair Jack Patrick Lewis. If you have any questions regarding the hearing, legislation, format of the hearing or reasonable accommodations required to ensure people with disabilities can participate fully in the committee process, please email: Geoff Richelew at (geoff.richelew@mahouse.gov). House Chair Lewis' office can also be reached via telephone: (617) 722-2582.

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
