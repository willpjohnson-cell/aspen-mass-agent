11:00 AM
Location:
A-2 and Virtual
Event Description
Gaming and Miscellaneous Matters

Please be advised that the schedule and agenda are subject to change at the agreement of the Chairs. 

*All House matters listed above are required to be reported on by December 17, 2025, subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27. 

 

Instructions for How to Register for Virtual or In-Person Testimony/Submission of Written Testimony 

Remote Testimony Registration: To testify remotely, please fill out this form.  For remote testimony, the deadline to register to testify is 4:00 PM on Tuesday, November 11, 2025. Those registered to testify will receive further instruction on joining the hearing remotely via email prior to the start of the hearing. 

 

In-Person Testimony Registration: For in-person testimony, individuals may fill out this form ahead of the 4:00 PM deadline on Tuesday, November 11, 2025 or sign up in person at the time of the hearing. Those signing up the day of the hearing will be asked to fill out a registration form outside of the hearing room. 

 

Legislators and Public Official Testimony: Legislators and public officials are required to register to testify at this hearing. The presiding Chair, at their discretion, may take legislators and public officials out of turn. 

 

Oral Testimony: Each individual registered to testify shall have 3 minutes to speak. Individuals offering testimony on multiple bills must do so within this 3 minute time frame. The presiding Chair, may further limit the time for testimony. 

 

Panel Testimony: Those wishing to testify as part of a panel shall fill out 1 of pre-registration form. Please have 1 member of your panel fill out this form with the information for all other panelists. Do not submit more than 1 pre-registration form for a panel.

 

Order of Testimony: The presiding Chair, at their discretion, will determine the order of testimony during the course of the hearing. 

 

Written Testimony: Individuals wishing to provide written testimony only do not need to complete a form. The deadline to submit written testimony is Thursday, November 20, 2025 at 5:00PM. For House bills, electronic written testimony may be submitted to Halle Mahoney at halle.mahoney@mahouse.gov. For Senate bills, electronic written testimony may be submitted to Zach Crowley at zach.crowley@masenate.gov. 

 

Written testimony may also be physically mailed to the House and Senate Chairs at the below addresses. 

 

Chair Carole Fiola 

Joint Committee on Economic Development and Emerging Technologies 

Attn: Halle Mahoney 

24 Beacon Street Room 236 

Boston, MA 02133

 

Chair Barry Finegold 

Joint Committee on Economic Development and Emerging Technologies 

Attn: Zach Crowley

24 Beacon Street Room 109-D

Boston, MA 02133

 

At the discretion of the Chairs and per committee rules, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. The committee may limit or redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness or safety of the testifier or others. 

 

Accessibility Accommodations: The committee will work to coordinate reasonable accommodations required to ensure that people with disabilities are able to participate fully in the hearing process. Timely requests for accessibility accommodations, such as assistive listening devices and systems, sign language interpreting services, closed captioning and transcription services, will be provided upon request. If you need an accessibility accommodation please indicate so in the form linked above. Do not hesitate to contact Halle Mahoney (halle.mahoney@mahouse.gov) with any inquiries or requests for special accommodations to participate in this hearing. 

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
