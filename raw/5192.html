Disability and Health presumption

Joint Committee on Public Service

PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE

Wednesday, May 28, 2025

1:00 P.M. – 4:00 P.M.

Hearing Room A2


 


Rep. Daniel J. Ryan

Sen. Michael D. Brady

House Chair

Senate Chair

 

PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE

 

The Joint Committee on Public Service will hold a hybrid public hearing to solicit written and oral testimony in-person and remotely on the following bills:

Disabilities/ Health Presumption

 

Please be advised that the schedule and agenda are subject to change at the agreement of the Chairs.

All matters filed in the House that are listed above are required to be reported on by [July26,2025], subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27.

The public is invited to participate in this hybrid hearing, which will be livestreamed on the General Court website. Hearings will be recorded and posted publicly on the Joint Committee on Public Service page.

At the discretion of the Chairs and per committee rules, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. The committee may limit availability or redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information, information about minors, or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness or safety of the testifier or others.

Written testimony can be submitted via email to Donna.loconte@masenate.gov or

Seda.korroch@mahouse.gov or mailed to the Joint Committee on Public Service.

Please submit by Friday, June 3, 2025 at 5:00pm:

CHAIR MICHAEL D. BRADY
JOINT COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC SERVICE
ATTN: AL DEGIROLAMO
24 BEACON ST ROOM 416A
BOSTON MA 02133

In-person oral testimony and remote testimony sign-up via email to Donna LoConte Donna.loconte@masenate.gov. Deadline to sign up is Monday, May 26, 2025 at 5:00pm.

For in-person and remote testimony please include:
1. Name;
2. Organization you represent;
3. Whether on behalf of yourself or an organization;
4. Mailing address;
5. Email address;
6. Telephone number;
7. In-person or remote testimony;
8. Bills testifying on;
9. Whether support or oppose.

For those testifying remotely, you will receive a Microsoft Teams link with more information prior to the start of the hearing.

This hearing will be chaired by Senate Chair Michael D. Brady. If you have any questions regarding the hearing, legislation, format of the hearing or reasonable accommodations required to ensure people with disabilities can participate fully in the committee process, please email: Donna LoConte donna.loconte@masenate.gov). Senate Chair Brady’s office can be reached via telephone: (617) 722-1200.

If you have Accessibility needs, click here.

 

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
