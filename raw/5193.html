Event Details

Status:
Completed
Event Date:
Monday, June 2, 2025
Start Time:
11:00 AM
Location:
Gardner Auditorium and Virtual
Event Description
Health Care Resources & Market Oversight; Pharmaceutical Access, Cost & Transparency

The Joint Committee on Health Care Financing will conduct a hybrid public hearing on Monday, June 2, 2025, from 11:00 A.M. to 3:00 P.M. in Gardner Auditorium on bills relating to Health Care Resources & Market Oversight; Pharmaceutical Access, Cost & Transparency.

This hearing will be chaired by House Chair Lawn and will include the matters listed on the docket below. Please be advised that all House matters being considered are required to be reported on by August 1, 2025, subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27.

Questions regarding hearing logistics or on House bills on the docket should be directed to House Chair Lawn’s office by contacting Keith Macfarland via email at Keith.Macfarland@mahouse.gov, or via telephone at (617) 722-2430.

Questions regarding Senate bills on the docket should be directed to Senate Chair Friedman’s office by contacting Adelina Huo via email at Adelina.Huo@masenate.gov, or via telephone at (617) 722-1432.

The public is invited to participate in this hybrid hearing, which will be streamed live on the General Court website, https://malegislature.gov/. Hearings will be recorded and posted publicly, archived on the Joint Committee on Health Care Financing webpage, https://malegislature.gov/Committees/Detail/J24/Hearings for the duration of the 194th Legislative Session.

Please be advised that the schedule and agenda are subject to change at the agreement of the Chairs.

All House matters above are required to be reported on by August 1, 2025, subject to extensions consistent with House Rule 27.

LINK TO PRE-REGISTER TO TESTIFY: For both in-person and remote testimony, please fill out this FORM.

***INSTRUCTIONS FOR REGISTRATION / TESTIMONY*** 

DEADLINE TO PRE-REGISTER: For both in-person and remote testimony, the deadline to register to testify is 4:00 P.M. Wednesday, May 28, 2025. Individuals registered for remote testimony will receive an e-mail prior to the start of the hearing with a link to join the hearing on Microsoft Teams. 

SAME DAY SIGN-UP FOR IN-PERSON TESTIMONY: Individuals who miss the deadline to pre-register for testimony may appear on the day of the hearing and sign-up to speak in-person on forms provided by committee staff outside of the hearing room. When all pre-registered individuals have been called to testify, the presiding Chair will then call any individuals who sign-up on the day of the hearing. 

LEGISLATOR AND PUBLIC OFFICIAL TESTIMONY: Legislators and public officials are also required to pre-register to testify. The presiding Chair, at their discretion, may take legislators and public officials out of turn. The committee will extend this courtesy to legislators and public officials only, and it will not be expanded to include other individuals or panels. 

ORAL TESTIMONY – TIME LIMIT: Each individual registered to testify shall be afforded 3 minutes to speak. Individuals offering testimony on multiple bills must do so within the 3 minute time limit. The presiding Chair, at their discretion, may further limit the time for testimony. 

ORAL TESTIMONY – PARTICIPATING AS A PANEL: Individuals participating as part of a panel must fill-out the appropriate section of the pre-registration form. Please have each member of the panel fill out the pre-registration form separately and completely. The presiding Chair will permit either fully in-person panels or fully-remote panels only. Questions related to the registration of panels should be directed to Committee Director Timothy O'Neill at Timothy.O'neill@mahouse.gov. 

WRITTEN TESTIMONY – DO NOT FILL OUT THIS FORM: For individuals wishing to provide written testimony ONLY, please do not fill out this form. The deadline for the submission of written testimony is July 25, 2025 at 4:00 P.M. Written testimony may be submitted electronically via e-mail to the following:

  • House Chair: John.Lawn@mahouse.gov
  • House Staff: Keith.Macfarland@mahouse.gov
  • Senate Chair: Cindy.Friedman@masenate.gov
  • Senate Staff: Adelina.Huo@masenate.gov

Written testimony may be physically mailed to the House and Senate Chairs at the below addresses:

CHAIR JOHN LAWN
JOINT COMMITTEE ON HEALTH CARE FINANCING
ATTN: KEITH MACFARLAND
24 BEACON STREET, ROOM 236
BOSTON MA, 02133

CHAIR CINDY FRIEDMAN
JOINT COMMITTEE ON HEALTH CARE FINANCING
ATTN: ADELINA HUO
24 BEACON STREET, ROOM 313
BOSTON MA, 02133

At the discretion of the Chairs and per committee rules, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. The committee may limit or redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness or safety of the testifier or others.

ORDER OF TESTIMONY: The presiding Chair, at their discretion, will determine the order of testimony during the course of the hearing. It is the responsibility of the individuals registered to testify to be prepared to speak when called upon by the Chair. In the event that an individual is called by the Chair and that person is not logged into the Teams platform, the Chair will move on and call the next individual or panel. 

ACCESSIBILITY ACCOMMODATIONS: The committee will work to coordinate reasonable accommodations required to ensure that people with disabilities are able to participate fully in the hearing process Timely requests for accessibility accommodations, such as assistive listening devices and systems, sign language interpreting services, closed captioning and transcription services, will be provided upon request. Requests for services should be submitted by completing the state house accessibility form, https://malegislature.gov/StateHouse/AccessibilityForm/. If you have any questions regarding a submitted request for accessibility accommodations, please email Keith Macfarland at keith.macfarland@mahouse.gov.

If you have Accessibility needs, submit an accessibility services request.
