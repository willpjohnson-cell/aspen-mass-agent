PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE

Date of Hearing: Thursday, May 7th, 2026

Time: 1:00 PM-3:00 PM

Location: A-2 and Virtual

 

The Joint Committee on Community Development and Small Businesses will be holding a hybrid public hearing on Thursday, May 7th, 2026 at 1:00 P.M. in Hearing Room A2.  Individuals who wish to testify in-person or virtually may register to do so by filling out this form by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, May 6th, 2026.

Testimony is limited to three minutes per person. A panel of two will be limited to five minutes and a panel of three will have eight minutes. You will receive a Teams invitation and further instructions on how to participate virtually before the hearing. Written testimony can be submitted via email to Nik Orellana at Nikolas.Orellana@mahouse.gov. When submitting written testimony, please send it as an attachment and use the following document title format:  

Bill# - Your Organization’s Name – Support/Oppose 

At the discretion of the Chairs, written testimony received by the committee will be made publicly available. The committee may redact testimony that includes sensitive personal information, information about minors, or information that may jeopardize the health, wellness or safety of the testifier or others.

This hearing will be chaired by Chair Vargas. If you have any questions regarding the hearing, legislation, format of the hearing or reasonable accommodations required to ensure people with disabilities can participate fully in the committee process, please email: Nik Orellana at Nikolas.Orellana@mahouse.gov
