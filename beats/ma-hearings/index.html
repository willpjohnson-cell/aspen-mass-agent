Rules

Each rule is evaluated against every archived page and returns one of three verdicts, or says why it cannot. Every rule this beat declares currently returns nothing at all, for the reason stated below.

notice-10-day

Joint standing committees must notify the Sergeant-at-Arms of a public hearing at least ten days before it is held.

notice-10-day has returned no verdict on any of the 643 archived pages, and cannot return a verdict against this source.

The ten-day clock in Joint Rule 1D runs from the moment a committee notifies the Sergeant-at-Arms. The Legislature does not publish that date anywhere on malegislature.gov, so the archive does not hold it and no amount of further collection will produce it. first_seen records when this platform first saw a hearing page, which is a different event. Measuring one against the other would produce a number that looks like a notice figure and is not one. The rule is kept because the requirement is real and the gap is worth stating.

Rule as published: Massachusetts General Court, Joint Rule 1D, as amended June 26, 2025: "Notification to the Sergeant-at-Arms for public hearings conducted shall not be less than 10 days prior to the time of such meetings." https://malegislature.gov/Laws/Rules/Joint

What the test actually measures: The rule runs on the date a committee notified the Sergeant-at-Arms. The Legislature does not publish that date, and this archive does not hold it. first_seen records when this platform first observed a hearing page, which is a different event and can only ever be later than the notification. A bounded first_seen therefore supports a claim about what was observable on the website, not a finding about the rule itself.

Exceptions the archive cannot see: Joint Rule 1D lets the ten-day requirement be suspended in an emergency by recorded vote of the committee. The site does not publish those votes, so a page failing this test may still be compliant.
